# Call Log Vault v1.0.9

A small design refinement to the appearance controls, nothing else.

v1.0.8 gave the theme row four options: Light, Dark, AMOLED and Auto. Four was one too many. The row is back to three: **Light, Dark, Auto**. Under it sits a new switch, **Use AMOLED for Dark**.

Switch on, the middle option becomes AMOLED itself: Dark renders true black, and Auto's dark side renders true black too. Switch off, Dark is the normal dark theme everywhere. Whatever look you had before this update, you keep after it: the update maps your current settings onto the new controls exactly.

Everything else is unchanged from v1.0.8 (manual delete, backup schedules, the works). Same 4 permissions, no internet, same vault and backup format. This release installs over v1.0.8 and keeps your existing vault.
