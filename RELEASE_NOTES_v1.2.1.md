# Call Log Vault v1.2.1

One fix, spotted by a user within the hour: in a person's timeline, entries older than yesterday printed the date twice ("Sun, 04 Oct 2026 · 09:10 PM · 04 Oct 2026"), because the row combined a full-date day label with a separate short date. The label already carries the date, so the duplicate segment is gone. Today and Yesterday rows keep their short date, since their labels do not include one.

Nothing else changed. Same 4 permissions, no internet, same vault and backup format. This release installs over v1.2.0 and keeps your existing vault.
