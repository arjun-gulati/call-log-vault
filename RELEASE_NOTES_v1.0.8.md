# Call Log Vault v1.0.8

The requests release. All three features in this version came from GitHub issues filed by users.

**Appearance Auto (issue #1).** The theme row in the drawer gains a fourth option, Auto. The app follows your phone: light in light mode, and your chosen dark flavour (Dark or AMOLED, whichever you picked last) in dark mode.

**Manual delete (issue #2).** In a person's timeline, long press any entry to delete just that entry, or use the delete all option to clear the whole number. Both ask for confirmation and cannot be undone. One line we are not crossing: the vault still never deletes anything by itself. Deleting is only ever a manual action by you.

**Backup schedules (issue #3, part 1).** A new Backup schedule setting in the drawer: Smart (the original behaviour), After every call, Every 6 hours, or Daily. Part 2 of that issue, watching the backup folder so two of your own devices can share a history through your own folder sync, is planned next. It will work the same way everything here works: no server, no account, the app has no internet permission at all.

Nothing else changed. Same 4 permissions, no internet, same vault and backup format. This release installs over v1.0.7 and keeps your existing vault.
