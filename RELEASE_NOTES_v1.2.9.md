# Call Log Vault v1.2.9

This release closes out GitHub issues #6 to #10, all five requested by a user of the app. Thank you for filing them.

Separate scroll positions (issue #6). The Call log and People pages no longer share one scroll position. Scroll deep into one page, switch to the other, and it opens exactly where you left it, and the first page keeps its place too when you come back.

Scroll to top button (issue #7). Once you have scrolled down a page, a glass pill appears at the bottom right. Tap it and the list glides back to the top.

Clear page titles (issue #8). The header now names the page you are on, "Call Logs" on the call list and "People" on the people list, so the two pages are never confused.

Fast scroll scrubber. A slim glass pill sits on the right edge of both lists. Drag it and the whole history scrubs quickly up or down, with a small bubble naming the date you are about to land on, so long histories are easy to travel. It grows while you hold it and settles back when you let go.

Trash Bin (issue #9). Deleted calls now go to a Bin first instead of vanishing. Open Bin from the drawer to restore a deleted call or delete it forever. Anything in the Bin deletes itself after 30 days. One honest limit: calls deleted before this version were kept only as a scrambled fingerprint, with no copy of the call itself, so those older deletes cannot be recovered. The Bin protects deletes from this version onward.

Temporary pause (issue #10). Pause vault from the drawer for 1 hour, 4 hours, until tomorrow, or until you turn it back on. While paused, the app saves no new calls, and the header shows the paused state. Calls made during a pause are never imported, not even after the pause ends and a later sync runs. The pause is respected by every sync and import path.

Same 4 permissions, no internet, same backup format. This release installs over v1.2.4 and keeps your existing vault.
